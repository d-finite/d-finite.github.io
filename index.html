    Mingleyang Li | 李铭乐洋

    I am a second-year undergraduate student at Peking University, majoring in Computer Science and Technology in the Turing Class. My research lies at the intersection of embodied intelligence and robot learning. I am interested in building agents that can perceive physical interactions, retain task-relevant experience, and predict how their actions reshape the world. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Hao Dong, and mentored by Yuran Wang and Ruihai Wu.

    Research

    My research explores how embodied agents can understand, remember, and predict the physical world through interaction. I am especially interested in world action models, memory-aware robot policies, and generalizable manipulation, spanning model design, benchmark construction, and deployment in simulation and the real world.
